## 2024-05-24 - Terragrunt Provider Caching Optimization
**Learning:** Terraform's `TF_PLUGIN_CACHE_DIR` can suffer from race conditions and redundant downloads during highly concurrent multi-module deployments (`terragrunt run-all`), which severely slows down CI pipelines.
**Action:** Use Terragrunt's native provider cache (`TERRAGRUNT_PROVIDER_CACHE=1` and `TERRAGRUNT_PROVIDER_CACHE_DIR`) to significantly speed up provider installation in multi-module setups and avoid corrupted cache installations.
